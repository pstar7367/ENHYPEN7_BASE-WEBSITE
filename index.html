About Us

OUR STORY

One family, one shared dream.

ENHYPEN7_BASE is a fan community created to support ENHYPEN and bring ENGENEs together. Through fan activities, projects, updates, and shared experiences, we aim to create a welcoming space where every member can contribute.

Our foundation is simple: respect the artists, respect one another, and keep our support rooted in love.

OUR MISSION
✦

Support with purpose.

To unite ENGENEs, encourage positive participation, and coordinate meaningful activities in support of ENHYPEN.

OUR VALUES
♡

Respect. Loyalty. Unity.

We value kindness, teamwork, inclusivity, creativity, and a shared commitment to supporting ENHYPEN.
